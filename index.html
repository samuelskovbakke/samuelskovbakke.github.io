Research

Tracing Effect Mismatches

Master's thesis - Aarhus University

Flix's effect system produces technically correct but often unreadable Boolean-algebra errors. My thesis works through three iterative approaches to turn those errors into diagnostics a developer can actually act on: effect-flow graphs, lattice-based analysis, and Zhegalkin polynomials. The work shipped back into the Flix compiler itself.
